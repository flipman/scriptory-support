Scriptory Support

This guide describes Scriptory 2.0, currently in development. Some features are not present in the older App Store version.

Get help

Email Seth Parsons at sethparsons6@icloud.com. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment details. Please avoid attaching your library unless needed to explain an import problem.

Common questions

How do passages move forward? In Learning, choose Start a new week when you are ready. Learning is week 0, Daily covers weeks 1–5, Weekly covers weeks 6–29, and Life starts at week 30. The menu also offers Undo last new week when available.

Can I use my preferred Bible translation? You can type or paste a passage and label its translation. The searchable translations are listed in Settings. Choose a default there, or choose None to hide lookup. Lookup availability does not imply that every translation is licensed for built-in search.

How do I practice a long passage? Open Life → Long passages, or Library → Long passages. Scriptory recognizes connected saved references across your library, including chapter boundaries. Pick a group and use Section, Build up, or Full passage. Entries with gaps or different translation labels stay separate.

What does the verse total mean? Library shows both saved passages and individual verses recognized from their references. Overlapping references count once, including copies in different translations. A custom reference that cannot be interpreted is identified as uncounted rather than guessed. Search and stage filters update the totals.

Do iCloud backups sync all my devices? No. Each device keeps its own library. Settings → Your library → iCloud backup saves dated snapshots, and you explicitly choose a snapshot to restore. Restore replaces that device's library after confirmation and saves a recovery copy first. Reminder and appearance settings stay on that device. Turning backup off keeps existing copies.

How do I keep another copy? Choose Settings → Export backup to Files. Keep the JSON file somewhere you trust. Import a library lets you preview and merge an export or an original Scriptory plist library. Keep your original file until you have checked the imported passages and review stages.

Why did a reminder not arrive? Check Scriptory's Daily reminder setting and your device's notification permission. Reminder changes save automatically. Focus modes, notification settings, and device conditions can silence or delay alerts.

How do widgets work? Add Scriptory from your device's widget gallery. Home Screen widgets show Learning passages; the next arrow appears when there is more than one. Tapping the passage opens Learning. Lock Screen widgets show the reference. Settings includes a widget setup and refresh guide.

Do I need to purchase reminders? No. Every feature is free. Support Scriptory contains optional consumable tips, with no subscription or feature unlock.

How is my data handled? Read the privacy information for the 2.0 preview.
