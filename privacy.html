Privacy information

This information describes the Scriptory 2.0 development preview and this support website. It is being reviewed before the 2.0 App Store release. The Bible lookup provider's log-retention details remain unconfirmed, as explained below.

Scriptory is developed by Seth Parsons. You can use Scriptory without creating a Scriptory account. Its features are free, with optional tips.

Your library and preferences

Scriptory saves your passages, Bible references, translation labels, review stages, and review progress on your device. It also saves preferences such as appearance, text size, practice position, and reminder time. These records make the app work; Scriptory does not send your saved library or review activity to a developer-operated server.

The app shares your Learning passages and the selected passage with its widget through storage shared between Scriptory and its extension on your device. Widgets can display passage text wherever you place them, including screens other people may be able to see. Remove the widget if you do not want passages displayed there.

Optional iCloud and Files backups

When you enable automatic iCloud backup, Scriptory writes dated copies of your library and review progress to your Apple Account's iCloud Drive. Copies include a generated device identifier to distinguish backups from different devices. Apple provides iCloud storage; Seth does not receive these copies or access to your Apple Account through Scriptory. Apple's privacy and iCloud terms apply to that service.

These are backups, not continuous synchronization of a shared library. Turning automatic backup off stops new automatic copies but keeps existing copies. Restoring a backup replaces the library on that device after confirmation and saves recovery copies first. It does not change that device's reminder or appearance settings.

If you export a library, Scriptory writes a JSON file to the destination you choose in Files. Exports include saved passages, progress, and the saved reminder preference. A file you export, share, or store with another service is subject to the access and retention settings of that destination. Scriptory reads an import file only after you select it, and shows a review before importing it.

Optional Bible reference lookup

When you search for a passage, Scriptory sends the reference you enter and your chosen lookup translation directly over HTTPS to bible-api.com. The provider also receives connection information, including your IP address, to deliver the result and operate its service. Scriptory does not send your existing library, review progress, email address, or Apple Account identifier with the search.

The provider documents IP-based rate limiting. Its public documentation does not currently specify how long request or connection logs are retained. Scriptory cannot promise that the provider immediately deletes those records. To avoid lookup requests, select None under Settings → Default translation and type or paste passages manually. Saved passages can be reviewed offline.

Tips and Apple services

Optional tips are processed by Apple's in-app purchase system. Scriptory receives transaction information needed to verify and acknowledge a tip, and keeps acknowledged transaction identifiers on your device to avoid handling the same transaction twice. Scriptory does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password and does not send purchase receipts to a developer-operated server. Apple handles payment records under its own policies. Tips do not unlock features, and you may tip more than once.

Reminders use the device's local notification system. They contain a general invitation to review, not your saved passage text. You can change or turn them off in Scriptory and manage notification permission in your device's Settings. Rating links open Apple's App Store.

Contacting the developer

If you choose Contact developer, your mail app opens a message that you can edit or cancel. The draft includes the app name, app version and build number, device model (including its hardware model code), and iOS/iPadOS version to help troubleshoot. You can edit or remove these details before sending; no unique device identifier or passage library is attached automatically. Sending it shares your email address, message, and any attachments you choose with Seth and the email services used to deliver it. Nothing is sent automatically. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and resolve the issue. Avoid including private library files unless they are needed to explain the problem.

Advertising, tracking, and analytics

Scriptory contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or tracking SDK. Seth does not sell your passage library or use it for advertising. Optional external services, including Bible lookup, iCloud, the App Store, and email, have their own data practices as described above.

Retention and deletion

Your library stays on your device until you delete passages, replace the library through a confirmed restore, or remove the app and its local data. Offloading an app can retain its data. Deleting a passage does not delete it from an older backup or a previously exported file. Widget storage can also retain a cached Learning copy until refreshed or the app and extension data are removed.

iCloud snapshots are retained until removed; Scriptory does not currently expire them automatically. Turn automatic backup off before managing existing backup files through Files or your Apple Account's iCloud storage tools. If you need help locating a copy, contact Seth. Removing a file may leave a recoverable copy in Recently Deleted, depending on the storage service. Files exports and device backups are separate copies: manage them in the destination or backup service where you saved them. See Apple's iCloud storage guidance.

Seth retains support messages for as long as needed to address the request and any related legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email. Seth cannot delete files held only on your device or in your private iCloud account, or erase Apple's purchase records. Lookup-provider log retention must be addressed with that provider.

This support website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics script, advertising, sign-in form, or contact-submission form. GitHub receives normal web connection information when serving these pages; its handling is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Clicking an external link or sending email uses the chosen service.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, email sethparsons6@icloud.com. Updates to this policy will show a new effective date. Material changes in the app's handling of personal information will be reflected in the app and its App Store privacy information.
